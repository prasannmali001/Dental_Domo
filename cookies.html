Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Lumora Dental Studio uses cookies and comparable digital storage technologies when you browse our website and use our appointment reservation tools.

What Are Cookies

Cookies are compact text files stored on your computer or mobile device that help our website recognize your browser, maintain session state, and deliver a seamless, personalized consultation booking experience.

Categories of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Clinical & Navigation Cookies: Required for the website to function securely, including calendar widget initialization and contact validation.
  • Analytical Performance Cookies: Gather anonymous statistics regarding page loading performance and interaction paths, helping us refine our digital studio experience.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your preferred viewing options, consent decisions, and contact form drafts.

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can adjust or disable cookies at any time via your browser settings. Please note that disabling essential cookies may impact certain interactive tools, such as the direct Cal.com booking modal.

Contact

If you have any questions about our use of cookies, please contact us at hello@lumoradental.com.
