## 1. Executive Art Direction Overview

The visual direction reflects the atmosphere of a **$2,000 custom agency project**—a boutique private dental studio blending high-end Scandinavian wellness architecture, Japanese minimalism, and warm European hospitality.

- **Atmosphere:** Serene, architectural, reassuring, prestigious, and clinically pristine.
- **Color Temperature & Palette:** Warm ivory (`#FAF8F5`), forest green accents (`#143D30`), brushed champagne brass (`#C5A880`), muted sage (`#6B8A78`), warm travertine (`#EAE4DC`), and soft natural daylight (`5200K`).
- **Universal Guardrails (Negative Prompting):** 
  `distorted teeth, unnatural fluorescent white teeth, uncanny valley smile, cartoonish expressions, fake clinical blue lighting, plastic skin textures, extra fingers, malformed hands, blurry dental instruments, watermarks, text overlays, logos, low resolution, stock photo cliches`.

## 3. Google Flow Generation & Export Workflow

If generating or regenerating assets inside Google Flow:

1. **Access Interface:** Open the authorized Google Flow / Google Workspace AI generation portal.
2. **Select Model:** Choose the **Imagen 3 (Photorealistic / High-Fidelity)** model engine.
3. **Configure Settings:**
   - **Quality:** High / Fine Art
   - **Aspect Ratio:** Select the corresponding ratio (`16:9` for landscape suites, `4:3` for scenes, `1:1` for macros/portraits).
   - **Guidance Scale (CFG):** `7.5` – `8.0` for faithful prompt adhesion without over-saturation.
4. **Copy & Paste Prompts:** Use the exact prompts above, including the Universal Negative Prompt in the exclusion field.
5. **Export Format:**
   - Export images at maximum resolution (minimum `2048 x 1152px` for hero/suites, `1024 x 1024px` for squares).
   - Convert or export to `.webp` or `.jpg` at 85% progressive compression.
6. **Deploy to Project:**
   - Save files directly to `f:\Rcpit\webiste2\assets\img\` using the designated filenames above.
   - All website templates (`index.html`, `about.html`, `service.html`, `blog.html`) are already wired to these exact paths!
