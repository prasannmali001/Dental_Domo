Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

At Lumora Dental Studio, your clinical confidentiality and personal trust matter to us as much as your smile. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or reserve a consultation, and how we safeguard your data with rigorous clinical standards.

Information We Collect

When you reserve a visit or contact our concierge, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, preferred appointment times, and any details you choose to share regarding your dental care or aesthetic goals. We also collect anonymous diagnostics about website performance to maintain a seamless experience.

How We Use Your Information

  • To schedule, confirm, and tailor your clinical consultations.
  • To answer inquiries and provide bespoke treatment guidance.
  • To send appointment confirmations, reminders, and exclusive practice advisories (only with your explicit consent).

How We Protect Your Health Data

We treat all patient and medical inquiries with strict clinical confidentiality under prevailing data protection standards. We never sell or distribute your personal data. Information is shared solely with certified clinicians directly involved in your care pathway.

Your Rights & Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information at any time by contacting our studio team at hello@lumoradental.com.

Contact Our Concierge

Questions regarding our privacy standards? Reach us directly at hello@lumoradental.com or telephone 0114 317 7002. You can also reserve an appointment directly via our online concierge booking page.
